## 🎯 1. Executive Summary & Intent Strategy

MP Sole® is an industrial footwear sole compounder and manufacturer established in 1990. The target audience includes:
1. **Shoe Factory Owners & Brand Managers (B2B Commercial Intent):** Looking for bulk suppliers of PU, TR, PVC Jelly, and Orthopedic soles.
2. **Footwear Designers & Tooling Engineers (Commercial / Investigation Intent):** Seeking mold tooling, custom Shore hardness compounding, and rapid prototyping.
3. **Retailers & Wholesalers (Transactional Intent):** Searching for wholesale pricing, MOQ, and sample runs in Karachi and across Pakistan.

## 📈 3. Keyword Density & Natural Readability Standards

1. **Avoid Keyword Stuffing:** Primary target keywords are naturally embedded in `<h1>`, `<title>`, `<meta description>`, and the first 100 words of body copy.
2. **Semantic LSI Variants:** Synonyms such as "outsole compounding", "injection molding", "footwear tooling", and "wholesale sole supplier" are dispersed across descriptive paragraphs.
3. **Regional Signals:** Contextual mentions of "Karachi", "Pakistan", and "Global Export" anchor local and national organic authority in Google Search Console.
